Dispatch & Booking Questions

How quickly can an emergency plumber arrive?
For active leaks and ruptured water heaters, our dispatch target is under 60 minutes throughout St. Petersburg, Tampa, and Clearwater. Our mobile service vans stay stocked with diagnostic tools and replacement parts.
Do you charge extra for nights, weekends, or holidays?
No. We use clear, upfront flat-rate pricing. You get a transparent written quote before work begins, with zero overtime markups for nights or weekends.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured in Florida?
Yes. Gulf Coast Plumbing is a Florida State Certified Plumbing Contractor under license #CFC059281 with comprehensive liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage.
What warranties do you provide on plumbing work?
We provide a 10-year comprehensive labor warranty on all whole-home repiping and main sewer line replacements, alongside standard manufacturer warranties on water heaters and filtration systems.
