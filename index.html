What kind of warranty or guarantee does Gulf Coast Plumbing offer?
We provide full manufacturer warranties on all installed units (such as Bradford White water heaters and Moen fixtures) along with our Gulf Coast Plumbing 10-Year Labor Warranty on qualifying whole-home repiping and sewer replacements.
Why should I choose Gulf Coast Plumbing for my plumbing project?
We are a local, family-owned Florida plumbing contractor. Our licensed technicians never cut corners, never push unnecessary repairs, and respect your home with protective boot covers, floor coverings, and thorough post-job cleanup.
What is the process for working with Gulf Coast Plumbing?
Every service starts with a comprehensive on-site diagnostic and a written upfront quote. Once you give approval, our experienced master plumbers complete the installation using premium fittings, perform full pressure testing, and provide you with maintenance guidance.
Do you provide 24-hour emergency plumbing in St. Petersburg?
Yes! We maintain an active 24/7 emergency dispatch line for critical situations such as burst water lines, overflowing sewage, failed shutoff valves, and commercial water heater breakdowns across Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.
What are the warning signs of a hidden Florida slab leak?
Warm spots on your floor tiles, unexplained spikes in your water bill, the continuous sound of running water when all taps are off, and damp spots along baseboards are critical indicators of a slab leak that require immediate acoustic testing.
