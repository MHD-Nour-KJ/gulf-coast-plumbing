# Gulf Coast Plumbing — Design System & Style Specification

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A high-impact, modern industrial craftsman aesthetic derived directly from the reference system ("Constrc") and tailored to Gulf Coast Plumbing's St. Petersburg / Tampa Bay identity. The visual tone balances precision mechanical engineering with approachable, dependable Florida service.

Surfaces alternate between warm light canvas (`#F8F9FA`) and obsidian dark sections (`#0E1117`), highlighted by Gulf Coast Plumbing's signature flame orange (`#FF5500` / `#FF5F15`), industrial steel blue (`#1B263B`), and brushed chrome accents (`#E2E8F0`).

Key visual signatures:
- Floating frosted glassmorphic pill navigation with tactile action buttons.
- Architectural ruler/measurement tape ticker dividers separating key content sections.
- Pill-shaped interaction controls with integrated circular arrow indicators (`↗`).
- Asymmetric 3-column "Who We Are" grid with 3D technical badge accents.
- High-contrast obsidian dark "Awesome Services" interactive tabbed showcase.
- Alternating photo showcase with floating metadata cards and protruding pill arrows.
- Interactive dual-layer Before & After transformation slider.
- Structured customer FAQ with expandable accordion pills and certification credentials.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Canvas White / Warm Gray:** `#F8F9FA` — Primary page body and light section background.
- **Pure Surface:** `#FFFFFF` — White cards, floating badges, and light interactive pills.
- **Obsidian Dark Canvas:** `#0D0F14` — Dark showcase sections and footer.
- **Charcoal Surface:** `#161922` — Inactive service pills and dark stat containers.
- **Brand Flame Orange:** `#FF5500` — Primary call to action, active indicators, and highlight pills.
- **Deep Navy / Iron Base:** `#162035` — Secondary brand anchor from company seal logo.
- **Charcoal Ink:** `#18181B` — Primary typography on light surfaces.
- **Muted Steel:** `#71717A` — Supporting descriptions and technical metadata.
- **Subtle Ruler Border:** `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` — 1px architectural lines and ticker tape dividers.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display:** `Cabinet Grotesk` / `Outfit` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` — Tight tracking (`-0.025em`), heavy weight (700–800), compressed leading (`1.15`).
- **Body & Controls:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` — Weight 400–600, relaxed leading (`1.6`), high legibility.
- **Casing:** Sentence case and clean Title Case. No shouting all-caps text.

## 4. Component Rules
- **Pill Action Buttons:** High border radius (`9999px`), internal padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`, housing an inner circular action glyph (`↗` in a 32px circle).
- **Service Tabs:** Dark charcoal pills (`#1A1D24`) transitioning smoothly to vibrant Flame Orange (`#FF5500`) when active.
- **Interactive Before / After:** Two-layer photo slider with a tactile central drag handle and real jobsite comparisons.
- **FAQ Accordion:** Closed pills with orange circle arrows; expanded card with dark circular arrow and clean animated disclosure.
- **Ruler Ticker Dividers:** Subtle technical architectural measurement ticks along top and bottom transitions.
- **Responsive Parallax:** Smooth hardware-accelerated parallax transform on full-bleed imagery without layout shift.
